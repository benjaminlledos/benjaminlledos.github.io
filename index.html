Presentation

Welcome to my webpage. My research areas are the calculus of variations, elliptic PDEs and geometric measure theory.

I am interested in the uniqueness, regularity and qualitative properties of solutions to minimization problems in the calculus of variations or elliptic PDEs, Gamma-convergence for phase fields and homogenization, shape optimization.

Since September 2024, I have been a maître de conférences (associate professor) at the Nîmes Université, working in the Laboratoire mathématiques, informatique, physique et applications (MIPA).

Previously, I was a postdoctoral researcher at the Institut de recherche en mathématique et physique (IRMP), UC Louvain, under the supervision of Heiner Olbermann.

I defended my Ph.D. in July 2023, supervised by Pierre Bousquet at the Institut de Mathématiques de Toulouse.

I am the organizer of the MIPA seminar.

This website gathers information and documents about my research, teaching activities and academic background.
